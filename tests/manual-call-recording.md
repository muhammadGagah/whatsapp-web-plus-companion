# Call recording validation

Run `npm run test:recording:native` for the WAV writer and native compilation checks. Run `powershell.exe -NoProfile -NonInteractive -Mta -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tests/test-recording-native.ps1 -Loopback` on a supported Windows machine to test process capture against the silent test process. These checks do not open the microphone or record a real call.

Installed NVDA validation is required before release. Start a test call with a participant who knows it is being recorded. Open Tools, WhatsApp Companion, Start call recording. Select the running WhatsApp channel and the microphone used by WhatsApp. Verify that NVDA announces the controls, Tab reaches every control, Escape cancels, and neither opening nor cancelling the dialog starts recording. Assign start and stop gestures through Input Gestures and repeat.

Use headphones. Start recording and confirm that the success announcement occurs only after both capture clients start. Speak and ask the other participant to speak. Stop from the menu or gesture. Verify that the announced WAV exists, opens in a player, has the remote voice on the left and local voice on the right, and stays synchronized over a long call. NVDA and another application's audio should be absent from the left channel. Other sounds generated by WhatsApp may be present.

On a test account whose effective PowerShell execution policy is Restricted (the Windows client default; check with `Get-ExecutionPolicy -List`), confirm that microphones load, a connected call is detected, recording starts, and a call shortcut can restore the compact call view.

Start recording from the NVDA menu while the call window is active and confirm that recording starts; the closing setup dialog must not count as a focus change. In a separate run, switch to another application while setup is checking the call. NVDA must say that the call window lost focus, and no recording may start. Press Stop during preparation and confirm NVDA says preparation is being cancelled, then that the recording was cancelled.

Verify the documented microphone behavior by muting in WhatsApp while continuing to speak. The independent microphone recording should continue. Minimize the call window, switch to the compact view, and let the connection reconnect, each for more than 30 seconds while recording; recording must continue. Then end the call without pressing Stop. Within about 20 seconds NVDA must announce that the call ended and where the recording was saved. Repeat and press Stop before the call ends.

Repeat with a non-default microphone and a Bluetooth headset. Change or disconnect a device during recording. Verify either continued valid capture or a failure announcement with the partial file location, never system-wide capture. Check unsupported Windows, disabled microphone permissions, a missing WhatsApp process, two ambiguous package roots, a read-only destination, and insufficient disk space. No start announcement should be emitted if initialization fails.

During a recording, lock the session, enter the secure desktop, unload the plugin, and exit NVDA in separate runs. Confirm that capture stops and the WAV header is finalized. Kill the helper in a separate test and check that an error is announced rather than success. Kill NVDA in another run and confirm that the helper stops when its parent disappears. Recording must not resume automatically after unlocking or reloading.

The recorder uses bounded packet buffers and checks its streams only while active. Observe NVDA responsiveness during a long recording and confirm there is no growing memory buffer. The four-hour limit prevents WAV size overflow.

API references: [Microsoft application loopback sample](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/samples/microsoft/windows-classic-samples/applicationloopbackaudio-sample/) and [IAudioCaptureClient GetBuffer timestamps](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/audioclient/nf-audioclient-iaudiocaptureclient-getbuffer).

Verify Start is disabled when the last check found no call, and stays enabled when the call state is unknown (Start then reports that it could not check). With the menu or the recording dialog open, confirm that a PowerShell probe runs at most about every 15 seconds and never two at once. A compact call is only a setup candidate and must pass the full connected-call check before capture. Repeat with Stable and Beta running together. Test full and compact views at 100, 150 and 200 percent DPI and on multiple monitors. During recovery, switch windows or move the pointer and confirm no stale click or capture occurs. Stop during the forced probe and queued start and confirm capture never begins. Verify status changes are announced once, not on each poll. Test each call shortcut: one invocation restores controls, a second invocation performs the action. Check held keys and Sticky Keys, and confirm Tab navigation after restoration.

Lock during the final setup check and then unlock. Inputs must become usable again without recording. Cancel during that check and confirm a late result never starts capture. When Start becomes disabled while focused, focus must move to Cancel, with Tab and Escape still usable. After the call ends during recording, Stop and the saved-file announcement must still work.

After this fix, test call shortcuts while WhatsApp is slow. NVDA must remain responsive and repeated shortcuts must not start parallel searches. Switch away or lock during discovery, then return. An expired action must not answer, decline, toggle, or end a call later. Check both full and compact call windows.

Lock or enter the secure desktop immediately after a recording setup or stopping message is scheduled. No queued setup message should be replayed across the transition, and nothing is spoken on the lock screen. After unlocking, NVDA must announce once that the recording stopped and where it was saved. Press Stop during preparation and confirm NVDA says the recording was cancelled, not that no call was detected.
